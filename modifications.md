**Current Observations (What Currently Exists)**

From the front page, the following features are well highlighted:

- **Journal branding and mission statement** clearly displayed, including PTI affiliation.
- **Navigation menu** with sections like _About_, _Publications_, _Indexing Metrics_, _Guidelines_, _Policies_ and _Announcements_.
- A **current issue section** showing some article titles with _PDF_ links.
- Links to login/register on a separate platform (app.jhst.org).

**Critical Issues / Missing Elements**

Below are the key areas where the JHST site is noted for improvements:

**1\. Peer-Review & Editorial Process Transparency**

- There is no visible explanation of the peer-review model (double-blind, single-blind, timelines, or reviewer assignment process).
- No clear editorial workflow is described (submission → review → revision → acceptance → publication).  
   Why important: Peer-review transparency signals scientific rigor and legitimacy.

**2\. Submission System**

- The main website itself does not show an integrated submission portal; it links to a separate app (app.jhst.org), but the flow is not explained or welcoming.
- There are no author guidelines posted directly on the main journal site (e.g., file formats, word limits, reference style, figures/tables).  
   Why important: Authors need clear, accessible submission instructions and tools on the journal website.

**3\. Metadata & Article Information**

- Article listings lack basic metadata:
  - No indication of author affiliations, ORCID IDs, or abstracts (visible on main site).
  - DOIs are not shown next to articles.  
     Why important: Metadata and DOIs are essential for indexing, discoverability, and citation.

**4\. Indexing & Metrics**

- The _Indexing Metrics_ section exists but does not show which recognized databases the journal is indexed in (e.g., Scopus, Web of Science, DOAJ).

**5\. Publication Ethics & Policies**

- There is no dedicated page (visible from navigation) for:
  - Publishing ethics (COPE guidelines)
  - Plagiarism and similarity screening policy
  - Conflict of interest, human/animal research policies  
     **Why important:** Clear ethical policies align the journal with global publishing standards.

**6\. Contact, Editorial Board & Governance**

- The site is missing a detailed _Editorial Board_ page with profiles, affiliations, and roles.  
   **Why important:** A credible editorial board strengthens trust and confirms academic oversight.

**7\. Structured Author Guidance**

- No detailed _Author Guidelines_ or _Manuscript Preparation_ instructions (formatting, referencing, etc.).  
   **Why important:** Standard guidelines are necessary for quality submissions.

**8\. Technical and UI/UX Improvements**

- Search functionality is minimal.
- Article PDFs may open without context or metadata.
- Navigation labels are generic and need clear purpose (e.g., "Policies" is too vague without subcategories i.e.: "Policies" could refer to:

**Publication Ethics** (COPE guidelines, plagiarism, data integrity)

**Peer-Review Policy** (single-blind, double-blind, review process)

**Open Access / Copyright Policy** (reuse, licensing, permissions)

**Conflict of Interest Policy**

**Correction & Retraction Policy**).  
**Why important:** Good UI/UX improves author and reader engagement.

**Recommended Improvements**

Here's needed highlights to align the site with international journal publishing standards:

- **Peer-Review Model Page**
  - Explain review type (e.g., double-blind)
  - Give typical review timelines and decision criteria
- **Rephrasing of roles:**

- **Managing Director:** Oversees the journal's daily editorial operations, manages scholarly correspondence, and provides informed recommendations to the Editor-in-Chief regarding manuscript acceptance or rejection.
- **Editorial Assistant:** Provides operational and editorial support to the Managing Editor in the administration of journal activities.
- **Section Editor:** Assesses the suitability of submitted manuscripts, manages the peer-review process, and submits informed recommendations to the Managing Editor.
- **Editorial Board:** Provides comprehensive strategic guidance to the Editor-in-Chief and Managing Editor aimed at enhancing journal quality, visibility, and indexing performance.

- **Dedicated Submission Portal**
  - Integrate a _clear, accessible_ online submission system
  - Provide login/registration links with instructions for both Editors, Reviewers, Authors and Publisher login details
  - Ensure the portal integrates with author dashboards
- **Complete Author Guidelines**
  - Manuscript formatting and structure
  - Reference style
  - Supplementary files, figures, tables
  - ORCID requirement
- **Article Metadata Display**
  - Show full metadata on article landing pages:
    - Authors with affiliations and ORCID IDs
    - Abstract, keywords, date published
    - DOI (once minted)
  - Ensure each article page has a **permanent stable URL**
- **DOI Implementation**
  - Clearly display DOIs on article pages and PDFs
  - Ensure DOI links resolve to landing pages
- **Indexing & Metrics Page**
  - List reputable abstracting/indexing services
  - Provide journal impact or other quality metrics if available
- **Publication Ethics & Policies**
  - Create pages for:
    - Ethical standards and COPE adherence
    - Authorship criteria
    - Plagiarism screening requirements
    - Conflict of interest
- **Editorial Board Details**
  - Provide names, affiliations, bios, responsibilities of board members
- **Improved Navigation & UI**
  - Organize menu items clearly (e.g., _Submit_, _For Authors_, _Editorial Board_, _Ethics_, _Archives_)
  - Add search filters (by author, article type, year)
- **Enhanced Discoverability**
  - Add social sharing buttons
  - Include RSS feeds, alerts, newsletter signup
